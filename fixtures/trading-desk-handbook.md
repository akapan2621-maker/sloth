# Trading Desk Handbook

## Risk management rules

Position sizing must never exceed 1% of equity per trade. Stop losses are
placed beyond market structure, never at round numbers. The maximum drawdown
tolerated before a strategy review is 8% over a rolling quarter.

## Gold price action framework

Support and resistance are defined by swing highs and lows. A breakout is
confirmed when price closes above the prior swing high on the H1 timeframe.
Invalidation sits below the last higher low.

## Session checklist

Confirm the spread is below 0.30 before entering. Log every closed trade in
the journal before the next session opens.
